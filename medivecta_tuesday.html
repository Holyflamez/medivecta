About

Science-driven.
Globally credible.

Medivecta was founded to solve a structural problem in life sciences: the most biologically relevant testing environments are fragmented, under-governed, and inaccessible to global sponsors.

We are a specialist CRO partnerships company built around established field relationships, rigorous scientific governance, and the regulatory expertise to make data from any geography globally submission-ready.

Field Reach

Pre-qualified network across global sites, SOPs harmonised to EMA, VMD, ECHA, WHO, and EPA standards.

Regulatory Depth

Expert advisory across veterinary medicines, vector control, and African NRA frameworks in every engagement.

Partnership Model

Long-term relationships, transparent governance, and a shared commitment to scientific rigour and sponsor success.

Our Mission

To build a world class scientific services delivery ecosystem connecting sponsors and manufacturers with credible, quality-governed laboratory and field testing partners, and ensuring every study delivers data that meets the highest global regulatory standards.

Our Vision

To be the world's leading CRO partnerships company — a trusted, multi-sector bridge between scientific innovation and regulatory approval, wherever in the world the research needs to happen.

Access

We operate where the biology matters — high-need, biologically active environments across global field sites. Sponsors gain governed access without building their own infrastructure.

Governance

GLP-aligned and WHO/EPA method-compliant studies across laboratory, semi-field, and full field settings. Data packages structured for WHO PQ, ECHA BPR, and EPA regulatory submissions.
